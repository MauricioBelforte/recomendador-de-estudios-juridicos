## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, authoritative, and contemporary atmosphere engineered for high-stakes legal matchmaking. It balances the gravity and prestige of tier-one law practices with the speed, clarity, and precision of modern digital workflows.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Tone:** Prestigious, decisive, institutional, and reassuringly robust.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Corporate Luxury. High structural discipline, razor-sharp typographic hierarchy, pristine slate surfaces, and selective warm amber and emerald accents that denote verification, pedigree, and immediate counsel.
- **Emotional Intent:** Clients should feel absolute confidence in discretion, compliance, and credentialing; partner firms experience an environment on par with bespoke private client portals.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional trust through deep, ink-tinted navies balanced by cold-pressed slate surfaces and high-value accents.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Midnight Navy. Anchors navigation bars, key headlines, primary actions, and formal framing.
- **Secondary (`#1E3E62`):** Steel Navy. Utilized for secondary structural containers, supporting cards, active states, and subheadings.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Warm Amber Gold. Represents accreditation, elite ratings, premium tiering, and focal action accents (with `#F59E0B` for hover/accent states).
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Cool Slate Canvas. Backing for all primary interfaces, preventing the clinical sterile glare of pure white while maintaining immaculate legibility.

### Semantic & Accents
- **Surface Pure:** `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, panels, and data surfaces.
- **Border Default:** `#E2E8F0` for crisp, hairline structural division.
- **Direct Connect / Verified Badge:** Emerald (`#25D366` / `#16A34A`) specifically allocated to instant communication channels (e.g., WhatsApp VIP counsel) and verified vetting seals.
- **Text Primary:** `#0B192C` (98% contrast against white).
- **Text Secondary:** `#475569` for legal disclosures, metadata, and timestamps.

## Typography

The type system blends the architectural structure of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent corporate framing with the neutral, hyper-legible text performance of **Inter** for evidentiary details, legal briefs, and firm analytics.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with controlled negative letter-spacing to yield high-density, authoritative headlines.
- **Body & Data:** Standardized on Inter with strict vertical metrics to ensure long-form intake questionnaires, jurisdiction breakdowns, and case documentation maintain zero optical fatigue.
- **Caps & Badges:** `label-caps` enforces high-tracking uppercase typography for firm tiers, bar admissions, and status pill badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a disciplined 12-column responsive grid system centered within a maximum canvas width of 1280px.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) margins. Ideal for dual-pane lawyer dossier views, side-by-side fee schedule comparisons, and multi-tier filter drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins. Side panels collapse into progressive sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) margins. Matching wizards transition to single-column sequential screens with anchored bottom actions.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8px base grid (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Spacing between decoupled case sections scales up to 64px (`4rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids exaggerated, hyper-diffused cloud blurs in favor of crisp architectural depth: hairline borders paired with weighted, high-definition shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Tables, Structural Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` hairline border and ambient anchor shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05), 0px 4px 12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards):** Bounded by 1px `#CBD5E1` border with focused elevation: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06), 0px 10px 20px -3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Match Drawers, Floating Direct Actions):** Hairline border in `#94A3B8` (opacity 40%) with high-density shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.16), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.06)`.
- **Accent Glows:** Verification and gold badges carry an inner inset hairline (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to simulate metallic or enameled finish.

## Shapes

To project stability, rigor, and judicial precision, the design system utilizes a controlled, compact radius scale (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Inputs, Buttons, Badges, and Small Modules:** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners that avoid bubbly or playful impressions.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Structural containment with just enough softness to sit modernly on slate surfaces.
- **Floating Modals and Large Overlays (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Exceptions:** Pill shapes (`9999px`) are strictly restricted to status tags, lawyer availability indicators, and verified credential chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Institutional):** Deep navy `#0B192C` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` border radius, subtle border `1px solid #0B192C`. Hover: `#1E3E62`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#D97706`.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** `#FFFFFF` fill, `#0B192C` text, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Direct Connect / WhatsApp CTA:** `#25D366` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold text, high-contrast crisp drop shadow. Hover: `#1EBE5D`. Used strictly for real-time firm consultation and intake routing.
- **Prestige / Retainer:** Rich gold `#D97706` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, for priority retainer matching or top-tier firm booking.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Header houses firm name (`headline-sm`), verified seal (`#25D366` or `#D97706`), and primary practice areas.
- Body integrates structured metadata lists with horizontal dividing rules in `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Vetted / Top 1% Firm:** Amber accent tint background (`#FEF3C7`), text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, `rounded-full`, typography `label-caps`.
- **Bar Verified:** Slate emerald tint background (`#DCFCE7`), text `#166534`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`.
- **Practice Area Chip:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#1E3E62`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `4px`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text & Select Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0B192C`, radius `4px`, padding `0 12px`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#1E3E62`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.12)`.
- **Error State:** Border `1px solid #EF4444`, ring `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square/circle, border `1.5px solid #94A3B8`.
- Checked state: `#0B192C` fill with white glyph check or solid dot.
